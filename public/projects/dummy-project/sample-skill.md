# three-part-postify

A tiny sample skill included to prove the technical-details layer can serve a
downloadable, runnable artifact. Swap this for real skills on real posts.

## What it does

Takes raw notes about a project and returns the identical three-part skeleton
every post on this site uses: **blurb**, **technical details**, **full
background story**.

## How to apply

1. Paste your raw notes.
2. Ask for "the three-part skeleton."
3. Review each part for voice and truthfulness before it ships — showing is the
   floor, and nothing publishes that the source material doesn't support.
