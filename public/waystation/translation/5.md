# Entry 5 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "The hallmark of form"

Pradhan page: ~1 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*kiṃ-lakṣaṇaṃ rūpam | rūpaṇa-lakṣaṇaṃ rūpam | tad dvividham | sparśena rūpaṇaṃ pradeśena rūpaṇaṃ ca | sparśena rūpaṇaṃ katamat | kara-caraṇa-pāṣāṇa-śastra-daṇḍa-śīta-uṣṇa-kṣut-pipāsā-maśaka-daṃśa-sarpa-vṛścika-ādīnāṃ sparśena vyābādhanam | pradeśena rūpaṇaṃ katamat | deśena rūpaṇam idaṃ ca idaṃ ca rūpam evaṃ ca evaṃ ca rūpam iti praṇihita-apraṇihita-ceto-vitarkeṇa pratibimba-citrī-kāratā ||*

## English

What is the hallmark of form? The hallmark of form is *rūpaṇa* — the way it registers. This happens in two ways: through contact, and through partitioning.

Through contact: the body getting hit or hurt by hands, feet, stones, weapons, sticks, cold, heat, hunger, thirst, mosquitoes, gadflies, snakes, scorpions, and the like.

Through partitioning: the mind — focused or wandering — picks out "form is here, form is there; form is like this, form is like that," and makes impressions from what it partitions.
