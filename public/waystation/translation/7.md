# Entry 7 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "The hallmark of perception"

Pradhan page: ~1 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*kiṃ-lakṣaṇā saṃjñā | saṃjānanā-lakṣaṇā saṃjñā | saṃjñā nānā-dharma-pratibimba-udgrahaṇa-[svabhāvā] yayā draṣṭa-śruta-mata-vijñātān arthān vyavaharati ||*

## English

What is the hallmark of perception? The hallmark of perception is recognizing. Recognizing works by grabbing the impressions of whatever is there. That's what lets us name what we've seen, heard, sensed, or otherwise known.

## Note

The [*svabhāvā*] in the Sanskrit is Pradhan's bracketed reconstruction — the surviving manuscript is uncertain at this word.
