# Entry 3 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "Why exactly eighteen elements / twelve gateways"

Pradhan page: ~1 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*kimupādāya dhātavo 'ṣṭādaśaiva | dvābhyāṃ deha-parigrahābhyām atīta-vartamāna-ṣaḍākāra-upabhoga-dhāraṇatām upādāya ||*

*kimupādāyāyatanāni dvādaśaiva | dvābhyāṃ deha-parigrahābhyām anāgata-ṣaḍākāra-upabhoga-āya-dvāratām upādāya ||*

## English

Why exactly eighteen elements? Because the six senses and the six objects together carry the six consciousnesses across past and present. Six senses plus six objects plus six consciousnesses — eighteen.

Why exactly twelve gateways? Because the same twelve — the six senses and the six objects — serve as the gateway for the arising of the same six consciousnesses we just counted in the eighteen.
