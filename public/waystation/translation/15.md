# Entry 15 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "The four great elements defined"

Pradhan page: ~1 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*pṛthivī-dhātuḥ katamaḥ | kaṭhinatā || ab-dhātuḥ katamaḥ | niṣyandatā || tejo-dhātuḥ katamaḥ | uṣṇatā || vāyu-dhātuḥ katamaḥ | kampanatā ||*

## English

What counts as earth? Solidity. What counts as water? Fluidity. What counts as fire? Heat. What counts as wind? Liveliness.

## Note

Xuanzang T.1605 renders *kampanatā* as 輕等動性 ("light-and-moving quality"), matching the Kosa-tradition gloss *laghu-samudīraṇa*. The English "liveliness" follows DSBC's *kampanatā* directly — the animating, quick-making quality.
