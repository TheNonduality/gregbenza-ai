# Entry 25 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "How the feeling-aggregate breaks down" (three further classifications + definitions)

## Sanskrit

*punaḥ sukhā kāyikī vedanā duḥkhā kāyikī vedanā aduḥkhāsukhā kāyikī vedanā sukhā caitasikī vedanā duḥkhā caitasikī vedanā aduḥkhāsukhā caitasikī vedanā sukhā sāmiṣa-vedanā duḥkhā sāmiṣa-vedanā aduḥkhāsukhā sāmiṣa-vedanā sukhā nirāmiṣa-vedanā duḥkhā nirāmiṣa-vedanā aduḥkhāsukhā nirāmiṣa-vedanā punaḥ sukhā gredhāśrita-vedanā duḥkhā gredhāśrita-vedanā aduḥkhāsukhā gredhāśrita-vedanā sukhā naiṣkramyāśrita-vedanā duḥkhā naiṣkramyāśrita-vedanā aduḥkhāsukhā naiṣkramyāśrita-vedanā ca || kāyikī vedanā katamā | pañca-vijñāna-saṃprayuktā vedanā || caitasikī vedanā katamā | mano-vijñāna-saṃprayuktā vedanā | sāmiṣa-vedanā katamā | ātma-bhāva-tṛṣṇā-saṃprayuktā vedanā | nirāmiṣa-vedanā katamā | tat-tṛṣṇā-viprayuktā vedanā || gredhāśrita-vedanā katamā | pañca-kāma-guṇa-tṛṣṇā-saṃprayuktā vedanā || naiṣkramyāśrita-vedanā katamā | tat-tṛṣṇā-viprayuktā vedanā |*

## English

Feeling can also be classified three more ways. As bodily or mental. As bound (*sāmiṣa*) or unbound (*nirāmiṣa*). As being based on greed (*gredhāśrita*) or being based on renunciation (*naiṣkramyāśrita*). Each pair takes the same three tones — pleasant, unpleasant, or neither.

What counts as bodily feeling? Feeling that arises with the five sense-consciousnesses.

What counts as mental feeling? Feeling that arises with mind-consciousness.

What counts as bound feeling? Feeling that arises with the craving for self-existence.

What counts as unbound feeling? Feeling that doesn't arise with that craving.

What counts as being based on greed? Feeling that arises with the craving for the five sense-pleasures.

What counts as being based on renunciation? Feeling that doesn't arise with that craving.
