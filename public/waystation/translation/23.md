# Entry 23 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "Form that arrives as a mental object" (*dharmāyatana-saṃgṛhīta-rūpa*)

Pradhan page: ~3 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*dharmāyatana-saṃgṛhītaṃ rūpaṃ katamat | pañca-vidham | ābhisaṃkṣepikam ābhyavakāśikaṃ sāmādānikaṃ parikalpitaṃ vaibhutvikaṃ ca |*

## English

What counts as form that arrives as a mental object? There are five kinds: form that arises when you analyze a thing down to its smallest parts, the form of open space, form that arises from taking a vow, form built from someone's constructed conceptual system, and form produced by mastery in meditation.

## Note

*Parikalpita* renders here the same as in Entry 19 ("form / teaching built from someone's constructed conceptual system"). Same Sanskrit term, different domain — speech in Entry 19, mental form here. Consistency carries forward across domains.
