Pradhan page: ~1 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*cakṣur-indriyaṃ katamat | catvāri mahā-bhūtāny upādāya cakṣur-vijñāna-āśrayo rūpa-prasādaḥ || śrotr-endriyaṃ katamat | catvāri mahā-bhūtāny upādāya śrotra-vijñāna-āśrayo rūpa-prasādaḥ || ghrāṇ-endriyaṃ katamat | catvāri mahā-bhūtāny upādāya ghrāṇa-vijñāna-āśrayo rūpa-prasādaḥ || jihv-endriyaṃ katamat | catvāri mahā-bhūtāny upādāya jihvā-vijñāna-āśrayo rūpa-prasādaḥ || kāy-endriyaṃ katamat | catvāri mahā-bhūtāny upādāya kāya-vijñāna-āśrayo rūpa-prasādaḥ ||*

## English

What counts as the ear-sense? It is the transparent form, derived from the four great elements, that is the foundation of ear-consciousness.

What counts as the nose-sense? It is the transparent form, derived from the four great elements, that is the foundation of nose-consciousness.

What counts as the tongue-sense? It is the transparent form, derived from the four great elements, that is the foundation of tongue-consciousness.

What counts as the body-sense? It is the transparent form, derived from the four great elements, that is the foundation of body-consciousness.
