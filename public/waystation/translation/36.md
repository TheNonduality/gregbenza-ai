# Entry 36 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "*Confidence*" (*śraddhā*) defined

Pradhan page: ~3 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*śraddhā katamā | astitva-guṇavattva-śaktatveṣv abhisaṃpratyayaḥ prasādo 'bhilāṣaḥ | chanda-saṃniśraya-dāna-karmikā ||*

## English

What counts as *confidence* (*śraddhā*)? Concerning what exists, what has virtue, and what is capable, *confidence* is full conviction, clarity of mind, and aspiration. Its function is to provide the basis for *wanting*.
