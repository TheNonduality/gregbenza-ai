# Entry 6 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "The hallmark of feeling"

Pradhan page: ~1 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*kiṃ-lakṣaṇā vedanā | anubhava-lakṣaṇā vedanā | nānā-vidhānāṃ śubha-aśubhānāṃ karmaṇāṃ phala-vipākaṃ pratyanubhavanti anena iti anubhavaḥ ||*

## English

What is the hallmark of feeling? The hallmark of feeling is *anubhava*, experiencing. One experiences the fruition of all kinds of past actions, wholesome and unwholesome.
