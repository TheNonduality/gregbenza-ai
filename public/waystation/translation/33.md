# Entry 33 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "Mindfulness" (*smṛti*) defined

Pradhan page: ~3 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*smṛtiḥ katamā | saṃsṛte vastuni cetaso 'saṃpramoṣo 'vikṣepa-karmikā ||*

## English

What counts as *mindfulness* (*smṛti*)? *Mindfulness* is the mind not losing hold of what is already in mind. Its function is to keep the mind from drifting.

## Note

DSBC has *saṃsṛte* — likely an OCR slip for *saṃstute*, the standard reading in Sthiramati's bhāṣya. Xuanzang's 串習 supports *saṃstute*. Functional sense identical either way.
