# Entry 12 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "The hallmark of eye-consciousness"

Pradhan page: ~1 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*cakṣur-vijñāna-dhātuḥ kiṃ-lakṣaṇaḥ | cakṣur-āśrayā rūpa-ālambanā rūpa-prativijñaptiḥ yac ca tasya bījam upacitaṃ vipāka-ālaya-vijñānaṃ tac cakṣur-vijñāna-dhātu-lakṣaṇam || yathā cakṣur-vijñāna-dhātu-lakṣaṇaṃ tathā śrotra-ghrāṇa-jihvā-kāya-mano-vijñāna-dhātūnām api lakṣaṇāni ||*

## English

What is the hallmark of eye-consciousness? Eye-consciousness is the cognition of form. Its foundation is the eye, and its object is form. It also includes the seeds of that cognition ripening in the storehouse-consciousness.

Ear-, nose-, tongue-, body-, and mind-consciousness work the same way.
