# Entry 14 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "How the form-aggregate breaks down"

Pradhan page: ~1 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*rūpa-skandha-vyavasthānaṃ katamat | yat kiṃ-cid rūpaṃ sarvaṃ tac catvāri mahābhūtāni catvāri ca mahābhūtāny upādāya || katamāni catvāri mahābhūtāni | pṛthivī-dhātuḥ ab-dhātus tejo-dhātur vāyu-dhātuś ca ||*

## English

How does the form-aggregate break down? All form is one of the four great elements, or something derived from them. What counts as the four great elements? Earth, water, fire, and wind.
