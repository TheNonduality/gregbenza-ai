# Entry 31 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "Wanting" (*chanda*) defined

Pradhan page: ~3 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*chandaḥ katamaḥ | īpsite vastuni tat-tad-upasaṃhatā kartṛ-kāmatā | vīryādāna-saṃniśraya-dāna-karmakaḥ ||*

## English

What counts as *wanting* (*chanda*)? When there is something sought, *wanting* is the wish to act on it, pulling together whatever the action involves. Its function is to provide the basis for taking up effort.

## Note

DSBC has minor variants *upasaṃhatā* / *upasaṃhitā* and *vīryādāna* / *vīryārambha*. Going with DSBC; Xuanzang's 引發 / 精進 fits either.
