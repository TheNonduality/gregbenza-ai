# Entry 9 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "The hallmark of consciousness"

Pradhan page: ~1 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*kiṃ-lakṣaṇaṃ vijñānam | vijānanā-lakṣaṇaṃ vijñānam | vijñānaṃ yena rūpa-śabda-gandha-rasa-sparśa-dharmān nānā-viṣayān vijānāti ||*

## English

What is the hallmark of consciousness? The hallmark of consciousness is *vijānanā* — distinguishing. Consciousness is how we tell apart the various objects: forms, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, and mental objects.
