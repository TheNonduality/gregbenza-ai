# Entry 10 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "The hallmark of the eye"

Pradhan page: ~1 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*cakṣur-dhātuḥ kiṃ-lakṣaṇaḥ | yena cakṣuṣā rūpāṇi dṛṣṭavān paśyati, yac ca tasya bījam upacitaṃ [vipāka-]ālaya-vijñānaṃ, tac [cakṣur-dhātu-lakṣaṇam] || yathā cakṣur-dhātu-lakṣaṇaṃ tathā śrotra-ghrāṇa-jihvā-kāya-mano-dhātūnām api lakṣaṇāni ||*

## English

What is the hallmark of the eye? The eye isn't just an eye. It's what you've seen forms with, and still see forms with. It's also the karmic seeds of that eye, ripening in the storehouse-consciousness (*ālaya-vijñāna*).

Ear, nose, tongue, body, and mind also have the same hallmark as the eye.

## Note

DSBC drops *vipāka* from the compound *[vipāka-]ālaya-vijñāna* here; Xuanzang has 異熟阿賴耶識 (confirmed intact in Entry 12's parallel construction). The English "ripening in the storehouse-consciousness" rests on reading *vipāka* present. DSBC also closes the passage with *tac cakṣuḥ* ("that is the eye") rather than the parallel *-dhātu-lakṣaṇam* that Entry 11 and 12 both carry; Xuanzang's 是眼界相 matches the later parallels, suggesting DSBC omission.
