# Entry 2 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "Why exactly five aggregates"

Pradhan page: ~1 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*kimupādāya skandhāḥ pañcaiva | pañcākāra-ātmavastu-bhāvanatām upādāya | saparigraha-deha-ātmavastu, upabhoga-ātmavastu, abhilāpa-ātmavastu, sarva-dharma-adharma-abhisaṃskāra-ātmavastu, tad-āśraya-ātma-sva-vastu copādāya ||*

## English

Why exactly five aggregates? Because the sense of self attaches in five ways.

The form-aggregate (*rūpa*) can be defined as: the physical body and the world it senses.

The feeling-aggregate (*vedanā*) can be defined as: the felt tone of each sense experience as it happens.

The perception-aggregate (*saṃjñā*) can be defined as: the recognizing and naming of what comes up in your sense experience.

The formation-aggregate (*saṃskāra*) can be defined as: the making of wholesome and unwholesome causes and conditions that shape what comes next.

The consciousness-aggregate (*vijñāna*) can be defined as: the knowing that supports the other four, claimed by the sense of self as its ground.
