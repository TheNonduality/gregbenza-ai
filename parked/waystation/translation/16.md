# Entry 16 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "Derived form"

Pradhan page: ~1 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*upādāya rūpaṃ katamat | cakṣur-indriyaṃ śrotra-indriyaṃ ghrāṇa-indriyaṃ jihvā-indriyaṃ kāya-indriyaṃ rūpa-śabda-gandha-rasa-spraṣṭavyānām ekadeśo dharma-āyatana-saṃgṛhītaṃ ca rūpam ||*

## English

What does derived form break into? It includes the five sense-faculties: the eye, ear, nose, tongue, and body. It also includes the five sense-objects, minus the great elements themselves: form, sound, smell, taste, and touch. And it includes form that arrives as a mental object.
