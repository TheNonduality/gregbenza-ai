# Entry 28 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "Intention" (*cetanā*) defined

Pradhan page: ~3 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*cetanā katamā | cittābhisaṃskāro manaskarma | kuśalākuśalāvyākṛteṣu cittapreraṇa-karmikā ||*

## English

What counts as *intention* (*cetanā*)? *Intention* is the active forming of mind: mental action. Its function is to drive the mind toward the wholesome, the unwholesome, or the neutral.

## Note

Cetanā opens the long caitasika list. The definition is two-part: *cittābhisaṃskāra* (the active forming of mind) glossed by *manaskarma* (mental action) — apposition rendered with a colon. The function clause uses *cittapreraṇa* (driving the mind), distinct from *cetayate* in Entry 27 which renders as "directs." Two Sanskrit verbs, two English verbs: cetayate = directs, prerayati/preraṇa = drives.

Triad here is the strict ethical *kuśala / akuśala / avyākṛta* (wholesome / unwholesome / neutral), matching Entry 8 — not the *kuśalatva / saṃkleśa / avasthābheda* triad of Entry 27.
