# Entry 32 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "Conviction" (*adhimokṣa*) defined

Pradhan page: ~3 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*adhimokṣaḥ katamaḥ | niścite vastuni yathā-niścayaṃ dhāraṇā | asaṃhāryatā-karmakaḥ ||*

## English

What counts as *conviction* (*adhimokṣa*)? Once something has been determined, *conviction* is what makes the determination hold just as it was determined. Its function is to keep from being swayed.

## Note

Xuanzang's 印持 ("seal-holding") concretizes *dhāraṇā* into a seal-stamping image. Not imported — it's a Chinese metaphor, not Sanskrit content, and would read narrower in English than the Sanskrit warrants.
