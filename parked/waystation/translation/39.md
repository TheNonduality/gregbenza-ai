# Entry 39 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "*Non-greed*" (*alobha*) defined

Pradhan page: ~3 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*alobhaḥ katamaḥ | bhave bhavopakaraṇeṣu vā anāsaktiḥ | duścaritāpravṛtti-saṃniśraya-dāna-karmakaḥ ||*

## English

What counts as *non-greed* (*alobha*)? *Non-greed* is non-attachment to existence or to the supports of existence. Its function is to prevent misconduct from arising.

## Note

First of the three wholesome roots. *Alobha* is defined entirely through *anāsakti* (non-attachment) — a wholesome factor named through what it isn't. First *caitasika* in the list to fire this way.

The function clause *duścaritāpravṛtti-saṃniśraya-dāna-karmakaḥ* fires identically across all three wholesome roots (*alobha*, *adveṣa*, *amoha*). In English, the *saṃniśraya-dāna* (basis-providing) machinery is redundant here because *duścaritāpravṛtti* (non-arising of misconduct) names the factor's own direct effect, not a structurally separate downstream caitasika. Where *saṃniśraya-dāna* grounds a different factor downstream (sparśa → vedanā, chanda → vīrya, samādhi → jñāna, śraddhā → chanda, hrī/apatrāpya → saṃyama), the basis-language stays. For the three wholesome roots, the function collapses to "prevent misconduct from arising." Locked here; applied to Entries 40 and 41.
