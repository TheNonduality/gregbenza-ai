# Entry 34 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "Concentration" (*samādhi*) defined

Pradhan page: ~3 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*samādhiḥ katamaḥ | upaparīkṣye vastuni cittasyaikāgratā | jñāna-saṃniśraya-dāna-karmakaḥ ||*

## English

What counts as *concentration* (*samādhi*)? When there is something being examined, *concentration* is single-pointed focus on it. Its function is to provide the basis for knowing.

## Note

DSBC has *vittasya* — OCR slip for *cittasya*. Xuanzang's 令心一境 confirms.
