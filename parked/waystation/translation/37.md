# Entry 37 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "*Self-respect*" (*hrī*) defined

Pradhan page: ~3 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*hrīḥ katamā | svayam avadyena lajjanā | duścarita-saṃyama-saṃniśraya-dāna-karmikā ||*

## English

What counts as *self-respect* (*hrī*)? *Self-respect* is the inward recoil at one's own wrongdoing. Its function is to provide the basis for restraint from misconduct.

## Note

"Self-respect" carries *hrī* as a standing wholesome factor whose activity is the recoil from one's own wrongdoing. Conventional Buddhist English is "shame," but in 2026 American register that word imports therapy-culture / toxic-shame baggage *hrī* doesn't carry. "Conscience" was tested — cognitively closer to the faculty but loses *lajjanā*'s affective recoil. "Self-respect" inverts surface polarity (positive standing trait rather than negative event) but accurately names the wholesome moral self-regard that *hrī* is and that produces the recoil-activity Asaṅga defines.

The structural pair *apatrāpya* fires next — same definition with *parato* (by others) replacing *svayam* (oneself).
