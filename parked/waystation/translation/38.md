# Entry 38 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "*Decency*" (*apatrāpya*) defined

Pradhan page: ~3 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*apatrāpyaṃ katamat | parato 'vadyena lajjanā | tat-karmakam eva ||*

## English

What counts as *decency* (*apatrāpya*)? *Decency* is the inward recoil at one's own wrongdoing as seen by others. Its function is the same.

## Note

Pairs structurally with *hrī* (Entry 37). Asaṅga's Sanskrit is a single-word change: *svayam* (oneself) becomes *parato* (from another). The English mirrors this — same verb, same object, "as seen by others" carries the perspective shift. Sthiramati glosses *hrī* and *apatrāpya* as the *lokapālau*, the two guardians of the world: the inward and outward sides of the same wholesome move. *Tat-karmakam eva* — "its function is the same one" — refers back to *hrī*'s function clause: providing the basis for restraint from misconduct.
