# Entry 30 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "Contact" (*sparśa*) defined

Pradhan page: ~3 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*sparśaḥ katamaḥ | trika-saṃnipāte indriya-vikāra-paricchedaḥ | vedanā-saṃniśraya-dāna-karmakaḥ ||*

## English

What counts as *contact* (*sparśa*)? When sense, object, and consciousness meet, the sense-faculty is changed. *Contact* is the defining of that change. Its function is to provide the basis for *feeling*.

## Note

DSBC has multiple OCR slips in this passage: "trikasakṣipāte" → *trika-saṃnipāte* (meeting of three); "indriyavipāra" → *indriya-vikāra* (modification of the sense-faculty); "vedanāsanniśrayadāna" parsed as compound *vedanā-saṃniśraya-dāna*. Xuanzang (T.1605) confirms all three readings — 三和合, 諸根變異, 受所依. Silent corrections.

"Meeting of three" expanded inline to "sense, object, and consciousness" — Asaṅga's audience knew the *trika* shorthand; ours doesn't. Functional expansion, not interpretation.

The grammar restructured: dense single Sanskrit sentence broken into three English ones, each carrying one move — the meeting, the change, the defining. *Pariccheda* (literally "cutting around" — *pari* + *chid*) renders as "defining" — the mind setting boundaries to the change so the rest of cognition can work with it. "Discerning" was tested and rejected as too passive; "defining" carries the cut-around quality of *pariccheda* in plain American English.

*Saṃniśraya* renders "basis" — distinct from *āśraya* ("foundation," Entry 17). The two terms are near-synonymous in this period but Asaṅga uses both; English distinction preserves the Sanskrit. *Saṃniśraya-dāna* in caitasika function-clauses is the load-bearing single-source support that the next factor rests on. Locked Entry 34; applied retroactively here.

This is the formal definition of *sparśa* as caitasika. Earlier appearances at Entry 5 (sparśa as bodily-impact mode of form's registering) and Entries 24/25/26/27 (saṃsparśa as the meeting-event at each sense generating the six aggregate-types) used the term informally. The earlier renderings stand.
