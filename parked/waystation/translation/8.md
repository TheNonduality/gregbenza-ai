## Sanskrit

*kiṃ-lakṣaṇaḥ saṃskāraḥ | abhisaṃskāra-lakṣaṇaḥ saṃskāraḥ | [abhisaṃskāra-svabhāvaḥ] yena kuśala-akuśala-avyākṛteṣu pakṣeṣu cittaṃ prerayati |*

## English

What is the hallmark of formation? The hallmark of formation is *abhisaṃskāra* — active forming. That's how it works: it drives the mind into wholesome, unwholesome, or neutral kinds of action.
