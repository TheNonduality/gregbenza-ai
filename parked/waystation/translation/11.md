# Entry 11 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "The hallmark of form" (as element)

Pradhan page: ~1 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*rūpa-dhātuḥ kiṃ-lakṣaṇaḥ | rūpaṃ yac cakṣuṣā dṛṣṭaṃ dṛśyate ca yac ca tatra cakṣur-dhātor ādhipatyaṃ tad rūpa-dhātu-lakṣaṇam | yathā rūpa-dhātu-lakṣaṇaṃ tathā śabda-gandha-rasa-sparśa-dharma-dhātūnām api lakṣaṇāni ||*

## English

What is the hallmark of form? The hallmark of form is everything seen by the eye — the whole visible world that the eye-sense takes in.

The same goes for sound, smell, taste, touch, and mental objects — each is its own sense field.
