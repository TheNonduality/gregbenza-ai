# Entry 35 — Chapter 1, Section 1, "Discernment" (*prajñā*) defined

Pradhan page: ~3 · Status: LOCKED

## Sanskrit

*prajñā katamā | upaparīkṣya eva vastuni dharmāṇāṃ pravicayaḥ | saṃśaya-vyāvartana-karmikā ||*

## English

What counts as *discernment* (*prajñā*)? *Discernment* analyzes the factors of what you're examining. Its function is to dispel uncertainty.

## Note

DSBC has *upaparīkṣya* where Entry 34 has *upaparīkṣye* (locative). Likely inflectional variant; functional sense identical.
