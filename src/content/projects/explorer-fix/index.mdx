The method was one loop, repeated: **photograph the hardware → show the AI →
get a hypothesis → test it → photograph the result.** The photos did the heavy
lifting — board labels in frame let the AI identify exact parts and pull the
right reference material; wide shots gave it context; re-shooting whatever the
AI named confirmed both sides were looking at the same thing.

What the photos and AI together got right: identifying the ripped U.FL
connector and missing solder pad, framing the repair-vs-replace decision
honestly, and guiding five teardowns. What they couldn't do: see the damaged
wire harness hiding underneath the obvious antenna damage — that took hands.
The most instructive moment of the whole repair was the replacement part *not*
fixing it.
