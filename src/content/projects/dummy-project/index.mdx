## Why this page exists

Phase 0 is Bones. The point isn't to say something interesting — it's to prove
the structure a hundred real posts will stand on. So this page is deliberately a
placeholder, and it earns its place by demonstrating three things at once:

- **The three-part skeleton renders.** You just read the *blurb* up top. The
  *technical details* sat in the box above, with two files you can actually
  download and one link out. This — the part you're reading now — is the *full
  background story*: the deep pool a visiting AI draws from.
- **Media renders natively.** The header image is a real asset served by the
  build. And here's a clean YouTube embed, loaded only when you click it:

## What "identical skeleton every project" buys us

Every project on this site fills the same three blanks. That sameness is boring
on purpose — it's what lets a future Claude session read a project folder, draft
the next post into the same shape, and publish it on Greg's approval. The
consistency *is* the automation surface.

Nothing here is precious. When the census (Phase 1) finds the first real story,
this file gets deleted and a true one takes its slot — same skeleton, real work.
